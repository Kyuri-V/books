Preface

The Book as Instrument

The Book of Two Ways is not merely funerary literature — it is a harmonic navigation tool encoded in symbolic logic. It aligns vibrational identity with archetypal geography (flame, bark, lake, gate) to transform the initiate into a coherent resonance pattern capable of traversing the thresholds of life, death, and godhood.

Each "way" — solar and Osirian — is not a dogmatic endpoint, but a tuning channel. The entire document functions as a field-shaping ritual, a frequency script that activates traversal through death not by physical geography, but by field resonance, symbol recognition, and intentional alignment.

The Book is not a map. It is an instrument of memory.
Not a narrative. A resonance.

Structural Analysis

The Zipper Structure: Three Traditions, One Current

Applying Zipper Theory, we observe that the Book contains three interlaced currents:

🔵
Osirian Current
Regeneration, judgment, anatomical reconstitution. The path inward and downward.
🌕
Thothian Current
Lunar starlight, inner cognition, precision, language. The mediating measure.
🔴
Solar Current
Radiance, utterance, command of time and fire. The path upward and outward.

Rather than presenting these as rival theologies, the text performs a sacred braid — a trinary resonance convergence. Like three tones forming a chord, these traditions zip together not by blending, but by alternating and resolving.

The text is a harmonic zipper between dual initiation modes — solar command and Osirian surrender — anchored by the lunar eye of Thoth. The result is a triadic cosmogenesis that culminates in the initiate becoming the field.

Section I

Threshold of the Circuit Solar

Spell 1029 — Threshold of the Circuit
May trembling befall the eastern horizon of the sky at the voice of Nut as she clears the ways for Re, before the Great One, so that he may make the circuit. Raise yourself, O Re! Raise yourself, O you who are in your shrine, that you may lap up the winds, that you may swallow the vertebrae, that you may spit out the day, and that you may breathe Maat.
Field Implication: This is the moment of field ignition. The initiate does not rise alone — they rise as the cycle. "Swallow the vertebrae": internalizing the spine of reality. "Spit out the day": creation through exhalation. "Breathe Maat": breathe alignment, truth, harmony.
Spell 1030 — Initiation into the Lotus Bark
Go down with them to the lotus-prowed boat at the dockyard of the gods, that I may take a bark therefrom, two lotuses at its ends, that I may sail in it with him as "Monkey-heart," and that I may act as pilot in it to that district of Nut, to the stairway of Mercury.
Field Implication: The bark is a frequency state. "Lotus at both ends": bidirectional vessel — past and future simultaneously. "Monkey-heart, the pilot": the playful inner self becomes the guide. "Stairway of Mercury": the path of Thoth — resonant ascension.
Spells 1032–1033 — Entourage of Flame
Entourage of flame. Entourage of flame. Entourage of flame. Entourage of flame. This flame which burns against you is that which is around Re. Be fearful! A lord of storm is the Re-bark. Join the flame!
Field Implication: Flame equals frequency integrity. The flame only burns the incoherent. "Wiped-face": an archetype of egolessness. "Spat on Apopis' wounds": rather than destroying chaos, they neutralize and transform it.
Spells 1034–1035 — Declaration of Passage
I am Re... greater than you. I have passed the Ways of Rosetau which are on water and land. These are the ways of Osiris. As for any person who knows this spell for going down by them, he is a holy one in the suite of Thoth. But as for him who does not know how to pass on those ways, he shall be taken by a stroke of death which is ordained, being a nonentity who has no Maat forever.
Field Implication: Those who know their pattern pass freely. Those who do not become out-of-phase echoes. "I am Re... greater than you": not arrogance — assertion of frequency match. Spell = pattern, resonance key. Knowledge = phase access.
Spell 1131 — Osirian Variant Osirian
I have come that I may see Osiris, live beside him, and rot beside him. I resemble you. I am your image. As for any person who will know their words, he will pass by there. He will sit beside the Great God. He is entirely perfect and spirit. He will not perish forever.
Field Implication: The Osirian path is not upward, but inward and downward. True passage occurs through decay into coherence. "I am your image": the initiate sees themselves in Osiris — a fractal likeness.

Opening of the Ways Triadic

This section marks the moment of initiation proper — when the paths split into upper and lower currents. The initiate traverses the celestial waterway on the bark of Re, passing demons, keepers, and flaming mounds.

Spell 1036/1147 — Opening of the Ways
I have come in the dignity of Shu. I have treated Osiris. Make way for me. Clap your hand! That which is in my hand is the best of the maces of Re. Depart!
Field Implication: Alignment with breath and balance. Shu = air, order, separation of realms. "Strike your forehead": awakening the eye of perception — the initiatory command.
Spell 1042/1151 — Spirit, Lord of Spirits
I am a spirit, the lord of spirits. The spirit which I have begotten, it exists; the spirit which I hate, it does not exist. I am one who goes around his lake in flame, the lord of light. I make the circuit while the eye of Horus is beside me. As for Thoth, he crosses the sky in my presence. I pass safely.
Field Implication: The deceased identifies himself as a "spirit" and claims power over other spirits. He accompanies Re during the day and Thoth during the night along this waterway which represents the sky.
Spell 1053/1153 — Way to Those Living on Sweets
I am the eye of Horus, excellent in the night, which makes flame with its beauty. I am lord of the horizon. If I pass, Apopis will really be overthrown.
Field Implication: The deceased identifies himself with the moon by becoming the "eye of Horus" excellent at night. Since he knows the demon names and the spell, light goes forth to open the firmament.
Spell 1054/1166 — Lake of Flame
Field Implication: This identifies the flaming lake which separates the two ways and which cannot be crossed. The Lake of Flame is the boundary between paths — the point of irreversible commitment.

Spell 1065/1168 — The Doors of the Cosmos

Spell 1066/1177 — Ram-Headed Threshold (Demon of Regret)

Section V

The Ways of Rosetaw Triadic

The Ways of Rosetaw are on both land and water — opposing directions that must be known to be navigated. The walls of flint are high. Only those who know can find their way.

Spell 1072/1182 — The Ways of Rosetaw
These ways are here in the opposite direction, each one thereof opposing its companion. It is those who know them who can find their ways. They are high on the walls of flint.
Field Implication: The ways oppose each other but continue one another. Opposition is not exclusion — it is complementary polarity. Knowledge is the key to navigation through contradiction.
Spell 1080/1087 — The Sealed Place
This is the sealed place which is in the midst of darkness. Flame is around it containing this effluvium of Osiris. As for any person who will know it, he can never perish there since he knows what will exist in Rosetaw. He sees Osiris every day. Breath is in his nose. He can never die.
Field Implication: The sealed "speech" in darkness, when known, guarantees life, breath, and daily vision of Osiris. The parallel text has "speech" for "sealed place" — if this speech is known, it is a guarantee of immortality.
Spell 513/577 — The Conclusion of the Short Version
Make way for me that I may carry off my enemy. I am Osiris hiding his throne, the Foremost of the Westerners. I am the lord of blood on the days of becoming. THIS BOOK WAS UNDER THE FLANKS OF KHNUM. Thus it concludes.
Field Implication: Version C ends with the deceased identifying as Osiris, prevailing over enemies. "This Book was under the flanks of Khnum" — the text itself is a sacred object, hidden within the creator-god.

Spell 1093 — Way of Thoth to the House of Maat
This is the way of Thoth toward the house of Maat. I shall be in the suite of Thoth at night towing them.
Field Implication: Navigation by inner measure. Maat as frequency, Thoth as guide.
Spell 1094 — The Rescuer of the Eyes
Hail to you, Thoth, who are in the suite of Re. I am the one who brought the dazzling sound eye. I am the one who removed bleariness from the injured eye that it might dazzle. I am the one who has saved Re from the raging of Apopis.
Field Implication: The initiate restores the sun and moon — "Brought the sound eye": balancing solar and lunar vision. "Cleansed the injured eye": restoration of psychic insight. The initiate is a restorer of resonance.
Spell 1095 — Isis the Guide
This is Isis who is before him in Maat. She shows him the ways in crossing the sky, that he may imitate what Re does.
Field Implication: Divine feminine as compass — intuition preceding logos. Resonant guidance comes from the inner Isis.
Spell 1091–1092 — Crown in the Flame & Thoth's Witness
I shall not burn while I am wearing the nemes or the white crown. Hail to you, Thoth, who was chosen as lord in early morning. I am in possession of Maat. Darkness is my abomination. I see the light. I open the darkness.
Field Implication: "I shall not burn while wearing the crown": truth transcends trial. "I see light. I open darkness.": the Logos in action.
Spell 1098 — The Coiled One (Re in the Night)
The one who shines in the night is Re. As for any person who is in his suite, he lives forever among the followers of Thoth. It is in the night that he is made to appear and Osiris is gladdened.
Field Implication: The journey ends not in escape, but in embodied light within shadow. The initiate does not flee the night — they shine through it. "Possessor of Joy": joy as signature of sovereignty.

The Codex closes here: not with a door, but a sun.

Seven gatekeepers, each with their own aspect and challenge, guard the pathways between life, death, and transcendence. They are not demons — they are field-bound initiatory functions, archetypes encoded at each turn of the spiral path. The gates are not obstacles, but interfaces of alignment.

Gatekeepers I–IV — Threshold Calibration (CT 1100–1103)

1. "The One Who Stretches Out the Prow-Rope" — Keeper of the Outside Gate. The initiate arrives from the great valley carrying knives.

2. "The One Who Cuts Them Down" — Keeper of the Second Gate. The Uraeus protects; Confusion stands opposite.

3. "The One Who Eats the Excrement of His Rear" — Keeper of the Third Gate. The initiate must know the name of the throwstick.

4. "Opposed-Face, Noisy" — Keeper of the Fourth Gate. Shu to the sky, Ruty to earth.

Field Implication: Each gatekeeper represents a specific polarity — outer darkness, inner confusion, sacred fire, distorted reflection. The initiate overcomes by truth-speaking and solar clarity.
Interlude — Solar Recognition (CT 1104–1106)
I came from the sky of Ruty, after I had preserved Maat. Make way for me that I may receive the great crown upon Horus, the eldest son of Re.
Field Implication: The soul is no longer merely resisting darkness — it is claiming the regalia of alignment.
Gatekeepers V–VII — Interior Guardians (CT 1107–1114)

5. "Upside-Down-Face, Numerous-of-Forms" — First inner gate. Lives on the demolishers who do not know how to pass.

6. "Who-Lives-on-Worms" — Middle gate. That which rots becomes fuel.

7. "Ikenty, Who Raises His Voice in Flame" — Third inner gate. Flame is on its mouth.

Field Implication: Elemental inversions — paradoxical mirrors meant to challenge perception. Each gate demands knowledge of the spell, affirmation of origin from Horus' sky, and integration of contradiction. Protection is always from the elder Horus.

Integration of the Traditions Triadic

This final section represents the harmonic convergence of solar and Osirian traditions, a reconciliation of inner polarity and a vision of the afterlife where Re and Osiris are not rivals — but aspects of the One.

The initiate is no longer passing gates or resisting demons. Instead, they enter the shrine of flame, are tested not by chaos but by the memory of it, and are counted among those who speak for Re and sit beside Osiris.

The Book offers three paths: Osiris (stillness, regeneration, the Field), Thoth (the lunar sky, the star-self), and Re (solar triumph, the voice that speaks creation). But the priest-scribes braided these traditions into a single current.

Knowing opens flame.
Naming commands wind.
Alignment grants immortality.

The final message is clear: all may reach the highest light. Even the commoner may become a star. Even Osiris returns to Re. What began as divergence becomes convergence. No single doctrine prevails.

The Book of Two Ways is not two. It is One Way remembered through two lenses.

The initiate walks not toward heaven nor underworld, but into the Field — the living memory of the cosmos itself.

To walk both paths is to no longer need them.
