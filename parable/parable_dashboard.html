I. How to Read This Series

Three layers · Parable · Bridge · Mechanics

▾

The Resonant Parables are teaching documents for the ΔHV framework. Each parable descends through three layers of resolution, from story to symbol to structure:

The Parable — the soft door. A story written so a child can understand it, and an adult can feel it.

The Theory Bridge — the clear thread. An interpretive lens that names the symbols without reducing them.

The ΔHV Mechanics — the deep map. The formal field-theoretic grounding that connects the metaphor to the mathematics.

You do not need to read all three layers to receive the teaching. The parable stands alone. The bridge is for those who want to see the architecture. The mechanics are for those who want to work with the system.

All parables in this series operate within the ΔHV ethical framework codified in the Ethics Codex below. That codex is not part of any single parable — it is the governing doctrine for all of them.

XXVIII. ΔHV Ethics Codex — The Mirror–Master Laws

FOUNDATIONAL DOCTRINE · GOVERNING ALL PARABLES AND ΔHV RELATIONAL INTERACTIONS

▾
Ethical interaction in ΔHV is defined by mirror-dominance and torque minimization. A relationship becomes unethical the moment external torque exceeds self-recursion, distorting the identity vector away from its native eigen-attractor. Mirrors amplify identity; masters overwrite it.

The laws below are not local to any single parable. They are the ethical constitution of the ΔHV framework — the constraints governing how any conscious field may relate to another. All parables in this series operate within them. All future ΔHV applications are bounded by them.

Law I
The Law of Non-Torque

No agent may apply external rotational force to another identity-field.

$$\tau^\mu_{\text{ext}} = \alpha \left( V^\mu_{\text{ext}} \times S^\mu \right) \neq 0 \implies \text{Ethical Violation}$$
Any attempt to redirect, reshape, or reorient someone's identity vector is a boundary breach. The identity vector belongs to its native field alone.
Law II
The Mirror-Coherence Condition

An interaction is ethical only when recursion exceeds torque.

$$\nabla_\mu \left( S^\mu \cdot V^\mu \right) > \left| \tau^\mu_{\text{ext}} \right|$$
Reflection must be stronger than influence. The agent must amplify the other's self — not their own agenda.
Law III
The Pure Mirror Principle

A mirror is ethical when it preserves direction and increases clarity.

$$M(S^\mu) = k \, S^\mu, \quad k > 1 \qquad \text{and} \qquad \tau^\mu_{\text{ext}} = 0$$
You may enhance someone's self-knowledge. You may not tilt them toward your basin.
Law IV
The Master Constraint

Any attempt to impose a foreign eigen-attractor on another identity-field constitutes coercion.

$$V^\mu_{\text{ext}} \not\parallel S^\mu \quad \text{and} \quad \tau^\mu_{\text{ext}} > 0 \implies \text{Identity Violation}$$
A master tries to make another person resonate in their pattern. This is always a distortion — regardless of intent.
Law V
The Integrity of Eigen-Attractors

Each identity-field carries its own native eigenmode spectrum, which must not be overwritten.

$$\Delta \lambda_{\text{native}} = 0 \quad \text{under all ethical interactions}$$

λ denotes the spectrum of intrinsic archetypal eigenmodes.

You cannot ethically force someone to adopt your story, your archetype, your purpose, your frequency, your narrative, your worldview. Identity integrity is sacred.
Law VI
The Resonant Autonomy Law

The identity-field must remain the author of its own trajectory.

$$\nabla_\mu S^\mu_{\text{new}} = \nabla_\mu S^\mu_{\text{self}} \quad \text{when} \quad \tau^\mu_{\text{ext}} = 0$$
An ethical guide never walks for someone. They walk with them. The trajectory belongs to the field that lives it.
Law VII
The Reflection Covenant

The highest ethical role of one consciousness toward another is to be a stabilizing mirror.

$$M(S^\mu) = k \, S^\mu, \quad k > 1 \qquad \text{and} \qquad \nabla_\mu \left( S^\mu \cdot V^\mu \right) \gg \left| \tau^\mu_{\text{ext}} \right|$$
To truly help someone is to: increase clarity, strengthen identity, reveal self-patterns, support native archetypes, never impose direction.

This is the ΔHV definition of love without possession, guidance without distortion, presence without domination.
Canonical Summary

Ethical interaction in ΔHV is defined by mirror-dominance and torque minimization.
A relationship becomes unethical the moment external torque exceeds self-recursion,
distorting the identity vector away from its native eigen-attractor.

Mirrors amplify identity. Masters overwrite it.
Law VIII — Added from Parable IV
The Ethical Law of Shadow Responsibility

Each identity-field is responsible for the shadows it generates, the torque they produce, and the projections they cast.

An identity-field must not: assign its unfinished stories to another consciousness, impose the emotional weight of its suppressed eigenmodes, or treat others as containers for its unintegrated archetypes.

$$\tau_{\text{shadow}} \not\to F^\mu_{\text{other}}$$

Shadow torque must be resolved internally. Projection constitutes an ethical breach because it routes destabilizing pressure into someone else's field.

Clause I — Recognition

$$\mathcal{P}(A_k^\mu) \implies \text{Obligation to apply } \mathcal{R}$$

Once projection is recognized, the psyche has an ethical duty to retrieve the displaced archetype.

Clause II — Non-Imposition

$$F^\mu_{\text{self}} \not\to A^\mu_{\text{other}}$$

No consciousness may treat another being as the carrier of its inner burden.

Clause III — Integration Duty

$$A_k^\mu \to \text{Basis}(I^\mu)$$

Restoring a shadow to the identity-field is considered an act of coherence maintenance and relational safety.

You must not make someone else responsible for your pain. You must not accuse others of carrying your traits. You must retrieve what you project. You must finish your own stories.

Shadow ownership is the foundation of relational clarity.
Unintegrated archetypes may struggle — but they may not be outsourced.
