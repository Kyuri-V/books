A Note to the Reader

This library is a system, not an anthology. The documents in it are not independent essays that happen to share a vocabulary. They are ordered expressions of a single foundational claim, each occupying a specific position in a hierarchy that has been made explicit. Reading them out of order is possible. Reading them in order is faster.

The foundational claim is this: reality is a continuous generative field whose distinctions are real as phase-stable organizations of relation. Everything else in the library — the mechanics, the metrics, the applications, the contemplative works — is a consequence, formalization, or expression of that claim. The claim is stated in Document I. Its vocabulary is locked in Document II. Its architecture is mapped in Document III. Those three documents are the entrance. The rest of the library is what you find once you are inside.

You do not need to agree with the claim before entering. You need only to understand it precisely — which is what the opening trilogy is designed to make possible.

Document I · Phase Realism

Document II · Phase Realism

Read this second. It locks the vocabulary the declaration depends on. Without this document, the vocabulary of the library remains poetic. With it, it becomes controlled architecture.

III
Architecture of the Framework
Document III · Phase Realism

Read this third. It shows where every other document in the library sits in relation to the foundational claim. Resonant Physics, Harmonic Realization Theory, ΔHV, and Convergent Coherence Analysis each occupy a distinct position in the hierarchy. The applied, cosmological, and expressive works are placed below them. After this document, the library has a shape.

Applied Works

If you want the framework running in specific domains — trauma, identity, relational AI, game dynamics, basin modeling, field event analysis.

Contemplative Works

If you want the framework inhabited rather than argued — parables, letters, threshold texts, and the sacred embodiment series. These are the library's lived register.

One thing this library does not do: it does not ask you to suspend disbelief at the door. The foundational claim is a philosophical position with specific consequences, stated in precise vocabulary, placed in explicit relation to adjacent positions it rejects. It is meant to be examined, contested, and tested — including by the challenge sentence that governs every formal document in the stack.

The challenge sentence: If a claim in this library cannot be expressed as a consequence, formalization, or application of phase-stable relational reality, it does not belong in the core framework. That sentence applies to every document in the formal and applied layers. It is not a constraint on the library. It is a clarification of what the library is.

Enter through Document I. The rest opens from there.
